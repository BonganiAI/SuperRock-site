About us

Built to your spec, in our own workshop

Established in 1978, Super Rock Drills designs, manufactures and supplies drilling rigs and earth boring equipment to the water-well, mining, exploration, piling and post hole industries — as well as specialised units for specific projects.

Our rigs are built to customer requirements and specifications, alongside our standard ROCKDRILL series. The entire range is designed and manufactured at our fully equipped workshops in Samcor Park, Pretoria.

Good to know

Frequently asked

Can you build a rig to my specifications?+

Yes. Our drilling rigs are built to customer requirements and specifications, and we build specialised units for specific projects alongside the standard ROCKDRILL series.

Truck or track mounted?+

Most models can be truck or track mounted depending on the application, with or without an onboard compressor. The ROCKDRILL 1000 is also available as a reverse-circulation rig.

Do you supply compressors?+

We offer sales and service of all the largest compressor brands in the country, as well as on-site training and technical information.

Which industries do your rigs serve?+

Water-well, mining, exploration, piling and post hole drilling.

Where are your rigs made?+

The entire range is designed and manufactured at our workshops at 187 Vonkprop Road, Samcor Park, Pretoria.
